Wordsim Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Wordsim is a word puzzle game published by Essenkay Studios. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

None. Wordsim does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal or usage data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no network access.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, scores and streaks are saved only on your device so you can continue playing. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

Wordsim is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects no information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
